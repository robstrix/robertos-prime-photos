About Me

Hey there, I’m Roberto Perez, a photographer serving the Bay Area and Central Valley. I’ve been capturing meaningful moments since 2013, and I truly love documenting the beautiful, creative, and compelling stories that make life special.

When you work with me, you can expect high-quality photography and a seamless, professional experience from start to finish. When I’m not behind the lens, you’ll find me exploring new places, trying local food spots, and spending time with my amazing wife, our two daughters, and our two energetic dogs.

Life is about making memories, and I’m here to help you preserve them.
