Let’s Make Your Grad Session Happen

With over 10 years of capturing meaningful moments, I’m proud to help graduating seniors celebrate this milestone with beautiful, timeless photos across the Central Valley and Bay Area.

I serve campuses throughout the region including Sacramento State, UC Davis, San Jose State, and more. Whether it’s your first grad session or you’re looking for something special, I create a relaxed environment so you feel confident and the photos show it.

Note: Travel fees may apply for campuses farther from my primary area. Reach out for details.

How to Book

1. Choose your package and preferred date/campus.

2. Submit the inquiry form or call/text 650.384.1078.

3. A $50 non-refundable deposit (Zelle or Venmo) secures your date within 24 hours.

4. Remaining balance is due on the day of the session.

Turnaround Time

Proofs delivered within 3–5 business days. Final edited images within 7–10 business days after you select your favorites.

Expedited options available upon request.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
3–4 months is ideal to secure your preferred date.

What if it rains?
We’ll check the forecast and can reschedule at no extra charge if needed.

Can I add family or friends?
Yes — packages are priced for one graduate. Additional people can be added; contact me for details.

Do you provide cap and gown?
You bring your own. Cap and gown photos are included in every package.
